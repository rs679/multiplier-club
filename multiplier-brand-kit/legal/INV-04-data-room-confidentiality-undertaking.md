# Data Room Confidentiality Undertaking

```
Document ID: INV-04
Version: 0.1 (draft for legal review)
Audience: Investors, and Authorised Users of Adviser Firms, opening a Product's data room
Presented when: The user first opens a particular Product's data room (once per Product)
Accepted by: click-through
Unlocks: Access to that Product's data room
Re-acceptance: On a material change to this undertaking; or if the Sponsor adds Product-specific confidentiality terms for that Product
```

> Drafting note: This is a short, per-Product undertaking. It supplements the confidentiality terms in the Investor Terms (INV-01, clause 14) and, for advisers, the Adviser Firm Agreement and Authorised User Terms. If a Sponsor requires its own non-disclosure agreement for a Product, the Platform should show it in addition to, or instead of, this undertaking, as set out in the Product Listing Schedule. Where both apply and they conflict, the Sponsor's agreement should prevail as between the user and the Sponsor.

## 1. Who this applies to

1.1 This undertaking is given by you, the person opening the data room, to Multiplier Private Markets Limited, a company incorporated in [PLACEHOLDER: jurisdiction] with registration number [PLACEHOLDER: number] whose registered office is at [PLACEHOLDER: address] (**Multiplier**, **we**, **us**), and to the Sponsor of the Product named above (the **Sponsor**).

1.2 If you open the data room for a company, trust, family office, fund or Adviser Firm, you give this undertaking both personally and on behalf of that entity, and you confirm that you are authorised to do so.

## 2. What is confidential

2.1 **Confidential Information** means all information and documents in or obtained from this Product's data room, or otherwise provided about the Product through the Multiplier platform at app.multiplier.club and any related websites, apps and services (the **Platform**), in any form, including summaries, notes, analyses and copies you make from them.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that:

2.2.1 is or becomes public, other than because you broke this undertaking;

2.2.2 you already lawfully had, without any duty of confidentiality, before you received it through the Platform; or

2.2.3 you lawfully receive from someone else who is not under a duty of confidentiality about it.

## 3. What you agree to do

3.1 **Use.** You will use Confidential Information only to evaluate whether to invest in the Product (or, for an adviser, whether the Product may be appropriate for your clients) (the **Permitted Purpose**).

3.2 **No forwarding.** You will not copy, download, print, forward, upload, photograph, screenshot or otherwise share Confidential Information outside the Platform, except:

3.2.1 where the data room settings allow you to download or print a document, for your own use for the Permitted Purpose; or

3.2.2 as allowed in clause 4.

3.3 **Security.** You will keep Confidential Information secure, keep your login details private and not let anyone else use your account.

3.4 **No contact.** You will not contact the Sponsor's portfolio companies, assets, tenants, borrowers, employees or counterparties named in the Confidential Information without the Sponsor's prior written consent. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether required.]

3.5 **Dealing restrictions.** If the Confidential Information includes non-public information about a company whose securities are listed or traded on a public market, you will comply with the insider dealing and market abuse laws that apply to you. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether to include.]

## 4. Sharing with your professional advisers

4.1 You may share Confidential Information with your own lawyers, accountants, tax advisers and financial advisers, but only:

4.1.1 to the extent they need it to advise you for the Permitted Purpose;

4.1.2 if they are bound by a professional or contractual duty to keep it confidential; and

4.1.3 if you tell them it is confidential.

4.2 You are responsible for any breach of this undertaking by anyone you share Confidential Information with.

4.3 You may also disclose Confidential Information where the law, a court or a regulator requires it. If you can lawfully do so, you will tell us and the Sponsor first, and disclose only what is required.

## 5. Watermarks and access logs

5.1 Documents in the data room may be watermarked with your name, email address, the date and time and other identifiers.

5.2 We record when you open the data room and which documents you view, download or print, and for how long. We share these records with the Sponsor and, if you are an investor with an Adviser, with your Adviser, as described in the Privacy Notice (PLT-02) and the Data Sharing Schedule (PLT-04).

5.3 You will not remove, hide or alter any watermark, or try to get around any access control.

## 6. Returning and deleting information

6.1 If the Sponsor or we ask, or if you decide not to invest, you will promptly delete or destroy any copies of Confidential Information you have downloaded or printed, and any notes containing it, and confirm this if asked.

6.2 You do not need to delete copies kept in automatic electronic back-ups, or copies the law or your regulator requires you to keep. Those copies remain confidential under this undertaking.

6.3 We may remove your access to the data room at any time, for example if the Product closes or the Sponsor asks.

## 7. No reliance on Multiplier

7.1 Confidential Information comes from the Sponsor. Multiplier does not check or confirm it, and does not make any statement or promise about its accuracy or completeness. We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product.

7.2 Nothing in the data room is an offer by Multiplier, or advice or a recommendation from Multiplier. Any offer is made by the Sponsor in its final offering documents.

7.3 You will rely only on the Sponsor's final offering documents and your own due diligence and advice when deciding whether to invest.

## 8. How long this lasts

8.1 This undertaking applies from when you accept it and lasts for [PLACEHOLDER: 2] years after your last access to the data room, or longer if the Sponsor's own confidentiality terms require.

9.2 We may also suspend or close your account under the Investor Terms (INV-01) or, for advisers, the Adviser Firm Agreement.
