# Adviser Points and Leaderboard Rules

> Drafting note for counsel: Points, rankings and rewards for individual advisers may be treated as non-monetary benefits or inducements under the rules that apply to Adviser Firms, and Regulators in several jurisdictions have raised concerns about "gamification" that may influence advice. Option A (product-neutral scoring) is recommended because it does not reward the sale of any particular Product or the fees generated. Option B (scoring linked to Deal Fees) reflects the owner's stated preference but must be reviewed by counsel in each jurisdiction before use, and may need to be switched off in some jurisdictions or for some firm types. Rewards should be designed to fall within the "minor non-monetary benefit" or equivalent category where such a category exists.

1.3 The points programme is a recognition and engagement scheme. It is not a remuneration scheme, and it does not affect the Adviser Share or any Override payable to your firm.

## 2. Taking part

2.1 **Opt-in.** Taking part is voluntary. You take part only if you opt in on the Platform and accept these Rules.

2.2 **Firm control.** Your firm's administrator may switch off participation for all or any of the firm's Authorised Users, or limit it (for example, to points without visibility, or without rewards). If your firm switches off participation, your participation ends and any unredeemed points lapse.

2.3 **Eligibility.** To take part you must be an Authorised User of an Adviser Firm whose Adviser Firm Agreement is in force and which is in good standing, and you must not be suspended from the Platform. Multiplier may exclude any jurisdiction, firm type or individual from the programme where it reasonably considers that participation could breach Applicable Law or a Regulator's expectations.

2.4 **Opting out.** You may opt out at any time in your settings. When you opt out, you are removed from the public leaderboard within [PLACEHOLDER: number] Business Days and your unredeemed points lapse [OPTION: unless you opt back in within the same League Period].

## 3. How points are earned

3.1 Multiplier awards points according to a scoring method that it sets and publishes on the Platform. The scoring method in force is:

[OPTION A: **Product-neutral scoring (recommended).** Points are awarded for activity that supports good practice on the Platform and is not linked to any particular Product, Sponsor, fee level or amount invested, such as:
- completing Platform onboarding, training and educational modules;
- keeping your professional profile and your firm's regulatory information complete and up to date;
- completing compliance attestations on time;
- inviting clients to the Platform who complete their eligibility and onboarding steps (whatever Products, if any, they later invest in);
- quality indicators, such as clients' completion of risk acknowledgements and the absence of upheld complaints, clawbacks or compliance flags; and
- giving product feedback to Multiplier.

No points are awarded, and no weighting is applied, by reference to any specific Product, Sponsor, success-fee rate, Deal Fees or amount invested.]

[OPTION B: **Score linked to Deal Fees (counsel review required before use).** Points are awarded in proportion to Deal Fees generated by Funded Investments of your Introduced Investors during the League Period, at a fixed conversion of [PLACEHOLDER: number] points per [PLACEHOLDER: amount and currency] of Deal Fees, displayed as points rather than as amounts. The same conversion applies to every Product, so that no Product or Sponsor earns more points than another for the same Deal Fees. Points are reduced if the Deal Fees are later subject to a Clawback Event. Multiplier may combine this with the product-neutral activities in Option A.]

3.2 **No points for prohibited activity.** No points are awarded for any activity that breaches the Adviser Firm Agreement, the Authorised User Terms, the Content and Marketing Guidelines (MPL-PLT-06) or Applicable Law.

3.3 **Corrections.** Multiplier may correct any points total that it reasonably believes is wrong, including because of a Platform error, a Clawback Event or conduct under clause 8.

4.1.3 are personal to you and cannot be sold, transferred, assigned, pooled or shared with any other person, including another Authorised User of your firm;

4.1.4 are not property and give you no right against Multiplier other than as set out in these Rules; and

4.1.5 lapse at the end of each League Period [OPTION: or after [PLACEHOLDER: number] months], when you opt out, when your firm switches off participation, when you stop being an Authorised User, or when your firm's Adviser Firm Agreement ends.

## 5. League periods

5.1 The leaderboard runs in league periods of [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. one calendar quarter] (each a **League Period**). Points earned in one League Period do not carry over to the next unless Multiplier says otherwise.

5.2 Multiplier will publish final standings for each League Period within [PLACEHOLDER: number] Business Days after it ends. Multiplier may adjust standings after publication to reflect corrections under clause 3.3.

5.3 Multiplier may run separate leagues, for example by region or firm size, and may decide which league you are placed in.

## 6. Visibility

6.1 **Separate opt-in.** Taking part in the points programme and appearing on the public leaderboard are separate choices. You can earn points and see your own position privately without appearing on the leaderboard.

6.2 **Percentile bands.** If you switch on visibility, other opted-in Authorised Users will see you in a percentile band (for example, top 1%, top 5%, top 10%, top 25% or other participants) rather than your exact score or rank. [OPTION: exact rank visible to other opted-in participants only]

6.3 **What is shown.** The leaderboard may show your chosen display name ([OPTION: full name / initials / alias chosen by you]), your band and, only if your firm has consented, your firm's name. It will never show any Investor's identity, any Product you have shared, any amount invested, or any Deal Fees or remuneration.

6.4 **Who can see it.** The leaderboard is visible only to Authorised Users of Adviser Firms who have opted in, and to Multiplier. Your firm's administrator and compliance users can see your points, band and any rewards you have received.

## 7. Rewards

7.1 **Non-cash only.** Multiplier may, at its discretion, offer non-cash rewards based on points or standings, such as invitations to educational events or professional development sessions, access to research or content, recognition badges or profile features. Rewards are never cash, cash equivalents, vouchers redeemable for cash, or payments.

7.2 **No entitlement.** Rewards are discretionary, and you have no right to any particular reward. Multiplier decides the type, value, number and timing of rewards and may substitute a reward of similar value.

7.3 **Your firm's and Regulator's rules.** Rewards are subject to the gifts, hospitality, inducements and conflicts policies of your firm and the rules of your Regulator. Before accepting a reward, you must check that you are allowed to accept it and obtain any pre-clearance your firm requires. Multiplier may ask for confirmation from your firm's compliance function before giving a reward, and may decline to give a reward to anyone for whom it may not be permitted.

7.4 **Limits.** Multiplier will not offer any reward with a value above [PLACEHOLDER: maximum reward value and currency] per person per League Period, and will not offer any reward that it reasonably believes could impair an Adviser Firm's ability to act in its clients' interests.

7.5 **No link to Products.** No reward is conditional on sharing, recommending or selling any particular Product, or on any Investor investing in any particular Product.

7.6 **Records.** Multiplier keeps a record of rewards given and will provide it to your firm on request, and to a Regulator where required.

7.7 **Tax.** You are responsible for any tax on rewards you receive, and must tell your firm about them where its policies require.

## 8. Anti-gaming

8.1 You must not:

8.1.1 create or use false, duplicate or shared accounts;

8.1.2 generate artificial activity, or encourage clients to take steps on the Platform solely to earn points;

8.1.3 share, recommend or encourage investment in any Product in order to earn points or improve your standing;

8.1.4 collude with any other person to inflate points or standings;

8.1.5 exploit any error, bug or loophole in the scoring method (you must report any you find); or

8.1.6 use any automated tool to interact with the programme.

8.2 If Multiplier reasonably believes that you have breached clause 8.1, it may remove points, adjust or remove your standing, disqualify you from the programme, withhold any reward or require its return (or its value), and tell your firm. A breach of clause 8.1 is also a breach of the Authorised User Terms.

## 9. Conduct and conflicts

9.1 You must never allow points, standings or rewards to influence any advice, recommendation or decision you make for a client.

9.2 The programme does not change your or your firm's obligations under the Adviser Firm Agreement, the Authorised User Terms or the rules that apply to you, including on suitability, conflicts and remuneration disclosure.
