# Data Sharing Schedule (controller to controller)

```
Document ID: MPL-PLT-04
Version: 0.1 (draft for legal review)
Audience: Adviser Firms (via their Firm Administrator) and Sponsors (via their Firm Administrator or authorised signatory)
Presented when: As a schedule to the Adviser Firm Agreement (MPL-ADV-01) and the Sponsor Listing Agreement (MPL-SPN-02), at the time that agreement is signed
Accepted by: e-signature countersigned by Multiplier (signed as part of MPL-ADV-01 or MPL-SPN-02; no separate signature)
Unlocks: The data flows described in clause 5 between Multiplier and the Adviser Firm or Sponsor
Re-acceptance: With any new version of the parent agreement, or when this Schedule changes materially (for example a change in transfer mechanism or a new category of shared data)
```

> Drafting note: This Schedule is incorporated into both MPL-ADV-01 and MPL-SPN-02 so that one set of data terms applies across the Platform. It assumes each party is an independent controller. Counsel should confirm that no flow makes Multiplier a processor for a Partner (or the reverse); if a Sponsor asks Multiplier to collect subscription data on its behalf in a form it dictates, a short processor addendum may be needed for that flow. The structure follows the UK ICO data sharing code and the EU controller-to-controller model; local equivalents should be checked for each launch country.

## 1. Status and interpretation

1.1 This Schedule forms part of, and is incorporated into:

1.1.1 the Adviser Firm Agreement (MPL-ADV-01) between Multiplier Private Markets Limited, a company incorporated in [PLACEHOLDER: jurisdiction] with registration number [PLACEHOLDER: number] whose registered office is at [PLACEHOLDER: address] (**Multiplier**, **we**, **us**) and an Adviser Firm; and

1.1.2 the Sponsor Listing Agreement (MPL-SPN-02) between Multiplier and a Sponsor.

1.2 In this Schedule, the Adviser Firm or Sponsor that is party to the relevant agreement is the **Partner**, and Multiplier and the Partner are each a **party**. The relevant agreement is the **Main Agreement**.

1.3 Capitalised terms not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Main Agreement or in the Platform Terms of Use (MPL-PLT-01), which relate to the Multiplier platform at app.multiplier.club and any related websites, apps and services (the **Platform**).

1.4 If there is a conflict between this Schedule and the Main Agreement about the protection of personal data, this Schedule prevails. If there is a conflict between this Schedule and a Transfer Mechanism (clause 12), the Transfer Mechanism prevails.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 In this Schedule:

2.1.1 **Data Protection Laws** means all laws relating to the processing of personal data that apply to a party in connection with the Main Agreement, including, where applicable, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the EU GDPR, [PLACEHOLDER: other applicable laws, e.g. UAE PDPL, DIFC Data Protection Law, ADGM Data Protection Regulations, Singapore PDPA, Malaysia PDPA, Brazil LGPD].

2.1.2 **controller**, **processor**, **data subject**, **personal data**, **processing** and **supervisory authority** have the meanings given in the Data Protection Laws (or their nearest equivalents).

2.1.3 **Shared Data** means personal data that one party discloses to the other under the Main Agreement, as described in clause 5.

2.1.4 **Permitted Purposes** means the purposes set out in clause 4 for the relevant flow.

2.1.5 **Personal Data Breach** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Shared Data.

2.1.6 **Restricted Transfer** means a transfer of Shared Data that, under the Data Protection Laws of the exporting party, needs a transfer mechanism or other protection to be lawful.

2.1.7 **Transfer Mechanism** has the meaning in clause 12.

2.1.8 **Platform Investor** has the meaning given in the Main Agreement and, for an Adviser Firm, **Client Investor** means a Platform Investor who is linked to that Adviser Firm on the Platform.

## 3. Roles of the parties

3.1 Each party is an **independent controller** of the Shared Data it receives and processes for its own purposes. The parties are not joint controllers, and neither acts as the other's processor, unless they agree otherwise in writing for a specific flow.

3.2 Each party is separately responsible for its own compliance with the Data Protection Laws in relation to the Shared Data it processes.

3.3 Nothing in this Schedule makes either party responsible for the other's processing, except as set out in clause 16.

## 4. Purposes

4.1 **Multiplier to Adviser Firm.** Multiplier shares data with an Adviser Firm so that the Adviser Firm can:

4.1.1 advise, support and communicate with its Client Investors about Products on the Platform;

4.1.2 carry out its own suitability, appropriateness and client due diligence obligations under its licence;

4.1.3 track its Client Investors' interest, subscriptions and investments made through the Platform;

4.1.4 reconcile Deal Fees, Adviser Share and any Override statements; and

4.1.5 manage its own Authorised Users.

4.2 **Multiplier to Sponsor or administrator.** Multiplier shares data with a Sponsor (and, at the Sponsor's direction, its administrator, transfer agent, escrow agent or registrar) so that the Sponsor can:

4.2.1 record which Platform Investors and advisers accessed a Product's data room;

4.2.2 assess, accept or decline a Platform Investor's subscription, including its own anti-money laundering, sanctions, eligibility and tax checks;

4.2.3 administer the Platform Investor's holding in the Product (including capital calls, distributions, reporting and investor relations for that Product); and

4.2.4 report closings and Funded Investments to Multiplier, and reconcile fees.

4.3 **Adviser Firm to Multiplier.** An Adviser Firm shares data with Multiplier so that Multiplier can:

4.3.1 invite and onboard the Adviser Firm's clients and Authorised Users to the Platform;

4.3.2 carry out firm verification (KYB) and screening of the Adviser Firm, its Authorised Users and payees;

4.3.3 operate the Platform, including access gating, records and audit trails;

4.3.4 calculate and pay the Adviser Share and any Override, and keep tax and accounting records; and

4.3.5 meet its own legal and regulatory obligations.

4.4 **Sponsor to Multiplier.** A Sponsor shares data with Multiplier so that Multiplier can:

4.4.1 carry out screening of the Sponsor and its key persons;

4.4.2 confirm the outcome of subscriptions, closings and Funded Investments, and calculate fees;

4.4.3 show Platform Investors and their linked advisers information about their holdings; and

4.4.4 meet its own legal and regulatory obligations.

4.5 Each party will process Shared Data it receives only for the Permitted Purposes for that flow, or for any other purpose that is compatible with them and permitted by the Data Protection Laws, or as required by law. Clause 11 sets out specific restrictions.

## 5. Data shared

5.1 **Flow A: Multiplier to Adviser Firm** (for Client Investors and the Adviser Firm's own Authorised Users only)

| Data subjects | Categories of data |
|---|---|
| Client Investors (and their representatives) | Name, email, phone, country of residence; Account status; investor category and eligibility status by country (not the underlying evidence unless clause 5.6 applies); identity verification status (pass, fail, pending); Products viewed and data rooms opened; interest indicated; subscription status and amounts; Funded Investments; investment information received from Sponsors (holdings, capital calls, distributions, valuations, statements) |
| Adviser Firm's Authorised Users | Name, email, role, log-in and activity records, acceptance records |
| Advisers introduced by the Adviser Firm (for Override purposes) | Name, firm name, date joined, aggregated Funded Investment and Override amounts, as set out in the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03) |

5.2 **Flow B: Multiplier to Sponsor or administrator** (for each Product, only for Platform Investors and advisers who take the action shown)

| Trigger | Data subjects | Categories of data |
|---|---|---|
| Data room opened | Platform Investor; linked adviser | Name, email, country, investor category; date and time of access; documents viewed; linked Adviser Firm and adviser name |
| Interest indicated or subscription started | Platform Investor (and, for entities, its directors, beneficial owners, signatories and controllers) | In addition to the above: identity and KYC/KYB data and documents; identity verification and screening results; eligibility declarations and evidence (MPL-INV-02); AML, tax and status declarations including source of funds and wealth, PEP status, tax residency, CRS/FATCA self-certification and US person status (MPL-INV-03); Product risk and subscription acknowledgement (MPL-INV-05); intended subscription amount; contact details |
| Ongoing | Sponsor's Authorised Users | Name, email, role, activity and acceptance records |

5.3 **Flow C: Adviser Firm to Multiplier**

| Data subjects | Categories of data |
|---|---|
| Clients the Adviser Firm invites | Name, email, phone, country of residence, and any other details the Adviser Firm enters to send an invitation |
| Adviser Firm's directors, beneficial owners, signatories, compliance officer | Identity and KYB data and documents, regulatory status and register entries, screening data |
| Authorised Users | Name, email, phone, job title, licence or registration numbers, identity data where required |
| Payees | Bank account holder name and details, tax and VAT details, invoices or self-billing information (MPL-ADV-06) |

5.4 **Flow D: Sponsor to Multiplier**

| Data subjects | Categories of data |
|---|---|
| Sponsor's directors, beneficial owners, key persons and signatories | Identity, professional history, regulatory history and screening data supplied under the Screening Application and Consent (MPL-SPN-01) |
| Platform Investors | Subscription acceptance or rejection (without reasons where the law prevents sharing them), closing dates, amounts, Funded Investment data, and investor reporting (holdings, capital calls, distributions, valuations, statements) |
| Sponsor's Authorised Users | Name, email, job title |

5.5 **Minimisation.** Each party will share only the Shared Data reasonably needed for the Permitted Purposes. Multiplier will not share with an Adviser Firm any data about a Platform Investor who is not a Client Investor of that Adviser Firm, other than as needed for Override reporting under clause 5.1.

5.6 **Reliance.** Where an Adviser Firm is relied on, or relies on Multiplier or a Sponsor, for client due diligence, any additional sharing of identity evidence will be set out in a separate reliance arrangement. [PLACEHOLDER: whether reliance arrangements will be offered.]

5.7 **Special category and criminal offence data.** Shared Data may include biometric verification results, PEP status and screening results that indicate alleged offences. Each party will process this data only where it has a lawful condition under the Data Protection Laws, and with additional protection proportionate to its sensitivity.

## 6. Lawful basis

6.1 Each party is responsible for identifying and documenting its own lawful basis (and, where relevant, condition for special category or criminal offence data) for:

6.1.1 disclosing Shared Data to the other party; and

6.1.2 its own processing of Shared Data it receives.

6.2 Where a disclosure relies on the data subject's consent or on a step the data subject has taken (for example a Platform Investor choosing to open a data room or subscribe), Multiplier will record that consent or step and make the record available to the Partner on request.

6.3 An Adviser Firm that enters a client's details to send an invitation confirms that it has a lawful basis to do so and that the client expects to be contacted about the Platform.

## 7. Transparency

7.2 Each Partner will provide its own privacy notice to the data subjects whose Shared Data it receives, as required by the Data Protection Laws, and will make it available to Multiplier on request so that Multiplier can link to it from the Platform where appropriate. 7.3 An Adviser Firm will tell its clients, in its own client documentation, that it uses the Platform and shares their data with Multiplier for the purposes in clause 4.3.

## 8. Accuracy

8.1 Each party will take reasonable steps to ensure the Shared Data it discloses is accurate and up to date, and will tell the other party without undue delay if it learns that Shared Data it disclosed is materially inaccurate.

## 9. Security

9.1 Each party will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the Shared Data it holds against Personal Data Breaches, taking into account the nature of the data and the risk to data subjects. The minimum measures are set out in Annex 1.

9.2 Each party will ensure that anyone it authorises to process Shared Data is bound by a duty of confidentiality and has received appropriate training.

9.3 Each party may use processors to process Shared Data, provided it has a written contract with each processor that meets the Data Protection Laws, and remains responsible for its processors.

## 10. Personal Data Breaches

10.1 Each party will notify the other party without undue delay, and in any event within [PLACEHOLDER: 48] hours of becoming aware of it, of any Personal Data Breach affecting Shared Data that it received from, or that relates to data subjects it shares with, the other party.

10.2 The notice will include, to the extent known: the nature of the breach; the categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records concerned; the likely consequences; the measures taken or proposed; and a contact point. Information may be provided in phases.

10.3 Each party is responsible for deciding whether to notify supervisory authorities and data subjects about a breach in its own systems, but will consult the other party in good faith before notifying data subjects where the notice concerns the other party, unless the law or the urgency of the risk prevents it.

10.4 The parties will cooperate reasonably to investigate, contain and remedy the breach.

10.5 Neither party will name the other in any public statement about a breach without consulting it first, unless required by law.

## 11. Restrictions on use

11.1 **Sponsors.** A Sponsor (and anyone receiving Shared Data at its direction) will:

11.1.1 use Platform Investor data received from Multiplier only for the relevant Product and its administration, as described in clause 4.2;

11.1.2 not use that data to market, offer or promote any other product, fund, vehicle or service to a Platform Investor, except through the Platform, for the duration of the Main Agreement and for [PLACEHOLDER: period, e.g. 24 months] after it ends, unless the Main Agreement expressly permits it (for example for investors on a pre-registered list of existing investors) or the Platform Investor has, independently of the Platform, asked the Sponsor to do so;

11.1.3 not contact a Platform Investor's linked adviser, or pay or reward any adviser, outside the Platform, except as the Main Agreement permits;

11.1.4 not sell, rent or otherwise disclose Platform Investor data to any third party, other than its administrator, service providers, auditors and regulators for the purposes in clause 4.2, or as required by law; and

11.1.5 not combine Platform Investor data with other data for profiling or marketing purposes.

11.2 **Adviser Firms.** An Adviser Firm will:

11.2.1 use Shared Data received from Multiplier only for the purposes in clause 4.1;

11.2.2 not use Product information or data-room access records to deal with a Sponsor outside the Platform, in breach of the non-circumvention terms of the Main Agreement; and

11.2.3 not disclose Shared Data to any third party except its own service providers, auditors, regulators and professional advisers, or as required by law.

11.3 Nothing in clause 11.2 restricts an Adviser Firm's use of data about its own clients that it obtained independently of the Platform.

11.4 **Multiplier.** Multiplier will:

11.4.1 use Shared Data received from a Partner only for the purposes in clauses 4.3 or 4.4 and for operating the Platform;

11.4.2 not solicit an Adviser Firm's Client Investors to bypass that Adviser Firm, to the extent set out in the client protection clause of the Adviser Firm Agreement; and

11.4.3 not sell Shared Data.

> Drafting note: Clause 11.1.2 must line up with the non-solicitation, tail-period and pre-registered investor carve-out in MPL-SPN-02. Counsel should check that the restriction is no wider than needed to protect Multiplier's legitimate interests and is enforceable in each relevant jurisdiction.

## 12. International transfers

12.1 Where a party makes a Restricted Transfer of Shared Data to the other party, the parties agree that the transfer is made under the mechanism set out in Annex 2 for that transfer route (the **Transfer Mechanism**), which is incorporated into this Schedule by reference.

12.2 Where the Transfer Mechanism is a set of standard contractual clauses, the parties agree that signing the Main Agreement also signs those clauses, with the module, options and annex details completed as set out in Annex 2.

12.3 Each party will carry out and document any transfer risk assessment that the Data Protection Laws require of it as exporter, and the importer will provide reasonable information to support it.

12.4 If a Transfer Mechanism ceases to be valid, the parties will cooperate in good faith to put in place a replacement. Until they do, the exporting party may suspend the affected transfers.

12.5 Onward transfers by the importer must be protected to at least the same standard.

## 13. Data subject requests and cooperation

13.1 Each party is responsible for responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights in relation to Shared Data that it holds as controller.

13.2 If a party receives a request that relates (in whole or part) to Shared Data held by the other party, it will tell the data subject how to contact the other party and, where helpful, forward the request to the other party within [PLACEHOLDER: number, e.g. 5] Business Days.

13.3 Each party will provide reasonable assistance to the other in responding to requests, complaints and enquiries from data subjects and supervisory authorities relating to Shared Data, including by providing relevant information within a reasonable time.

13.4 If a data subject asks Multiplier to erase or restrict their data, and the request affects the Partner (for example a request to stop sharing with a Sponsor), Multiplier will tell the Partner so that it can consider its own position. Each party decides for itself whether it must comply, taking into account its own retention obligations.

13.5 Each party will tell the other promptly about any complaint, investigation or enquiry from a supervisory authority that relates to the other party's processing of Shared Data, unless the law prevents it.

## 14. Retention and deletion

14.1 Each party will keep Shared Data no longer than necessary for the Permitted Purposes, and for any longer period required by law (including anti-money laundering, tax, financial services and record-keeping laws, which often require 5 to 7 years or more).

14.2 On termination of the Main Agreement, each party will continue to protect the Shared Data it holds under this Schedule for as long as it keeps it, and will delete or anonymise it at the end of its retention period.

14.3 A Sponsor may keep Platform Investor data for Investors who have become investors in its Product for as long as needed to administer that holding and meet its legal obligations. It will delete or anonymise data relating to Platform Investors who did not invest within [PLACEHOLDER: period, e.g. 12 months] after their last interaction with the Product, unless the law requires it to keep that data.

## 16. Liability

16.1 Each party is liable for its own breach of this Schedule and of the Data Protection Laws, and for the acts and omissions of its processors and staff.

16.2 Each party will compensate the other for losses, fines, penalties, compensation paid to data subjects and reasonable costs that the other suffers as a result of the first party's breach of this Schedule or of the Data Protection Laws, except to the extent the other party caused or contributed to them. Any fine or penalty is recoverable only where the law allows.

16.3 The limits and exclusions of liability in the Main Agreement apply to this Schedule, except that [OPTION A: liability under clause 16.2 is subject to a separate cap of [PLACEHOLDER: amount or multiple of fees] / OPTION B: liability under clause 16.2 is not subject to the cap in the Main Agreement / OPTION C: liability under clause 16.2 is subject to the cap in the Main Agreement without change]. Nothing in this Schedule limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for fraud.

16.4 Where the parties are jointly liable to a data subject under the Data Protection Laws, each may recover from the other the part of the compensation corresponding to the other's responsibility for the damage.

## 17. Term, changes and survival

17.1 This Schedule applies for as long as the Main Agreement is in force and afterwards for as long as either party holds Shared Data received under it.

17.2 Multiplier may update this Schedule to reflect changes in the Data Protection Laws, a Transfer Mechanism, guidance from a supervisory authority, or the data flows on the Platform. Multiplier will give the Partner at least [PLACEHOLDER: notice period, e.g. 30] days' notice of any material change, unless a shorter period is required by law. Re-acceptance will be requested as set out in the header block.

17.3 Clauses 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 and 16 survive termination of the Main Agreement.

## Annex 1: Minimum security measures

Each party will maintain at least the following measures, appropriate to the risk:

1. **Governance:** a named person responsible for information security and data protection; written information security and data protection policies reviewed at least annually.
2. **Access control:** role-based access on a need-to-know basis; unique user accounts; multi-factor authentication for all access to systems holding Shared Data; prompt removal of access for leavers; periodic access reviews (at least [PLACEHOLDER: frequency, e.g. every 6 months]).
3. **Encryption:** encryption of Shared Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest using industry-standard algorithms; secure key management.
4. **Network and endpoint security:** firewalls, malware protection, timely security patching, device encryption and screen locking on staff devices, remote wipe for mobile devices.
5. **Logging and monitoring:** logging of access to Shared Data; monitoring for suspicious activity; log retention for at least [PLACEHOLDER: period, e.g. 12 months].
6. **Secure development and testing (Multiplier):** secure coding practices, code review, separation of production and test environments, no use of real Shared Data in testing unless protected to production standard, and independent penetration testing at least [PLACEHOLDER: frequency, e.g. annually].
7. **Resilience:** regular backups, tested restoration, and business continuity and disaster recovery plans.
8. **Vendor management:** due diligence and written contracts with processors; review of processors' security.
9. **People:** background checks proportionate to role and local law; confidentiality obligations; data protection and security training at onboarding and at least annually.
10. **Incident response:** a documented incident response procedure that supports the notification timescale in clause 10.
11. **Physical security:** controlled access to premises and equipment where Shared Data is processed.
12. **Data handling:** no downloading of data-room Content or Platform Investor data to personal devices or personal email; secure disposal of data and media.
