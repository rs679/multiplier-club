## 1. Interpretation

1.1 This certificate is given under the Sponsor Listing Agreement (SPN-02) and the Product Listing Schedule (SPN-03) for the Product. Terms defined in SPN-02 have the same meaning here.

1.2 **Reference Date** means: for an initial certificate, the date of the latest audited financial statements of the Issuer (or, if none, the date of the Offering Documents); for any later certificate, the date of the previous certificate.

1.3 **Disclosure Annex** means Annex B. A matter is disclosed only if it is set out in the Disclosure Annex fairly and with enough detail to allow Multiplier to understand its nature and scope. General or deemed disclosures (for example of public records or of the whole data room) are not disclosures for this certificate.

## 2. Certifications

The Sponsor certifies to Multiplier that, as at the date of this certificate, except as disclosed in the Disclosure Annex:

### Status and authority

2.1 The Sponsor and the Issuer are duly incorporated or formed, validly existing and (where the concept applies) in good standing, and have the power and authority to carry on their business, to issue and offer the Product, and to perform their obligations under SPN-02 and SPN-03.

2.2 The Sponsor and each relevant member of the Sponsor Group hold, and comply with the conditions of, every licence, authorisation, permission, registration and exemption required for their business, for the Product and for making the Product available through the Platform in each Eligible Country. The regulatory status information given to Multiplier is current and complete.

### Documents

2.3 The Offering Documents and other Product Materials listed in Annex A are the current versions, are the versions uploaded to the Platform, and are true, accurate, complete and not misleading (including by omission).

2.4 The Offering Documents contain all information that investors and their advisers would reasonably require, and reasonably expect to find there, to make an informed assessment of the Product, including its risks, fees, costs, conflicts of interest and the rights attaching to it.

2.5 All forecasts, projections, target returns and statements of opinion or intention in the Product Materials are made honestly, on reasonable grounds and on assumptions that are disclosed, and remain reasonable. All performance and track-record information states its basis and is presented fairly.

2.6 Where Applicable Law requires the Product Materials to be signed off by an authorised or regulated person, they have been.

### No material adverse change

2.7 Since the Reference Date, there has been no material adverse change in the business, financial position, assets, prospects or regulatory status of the Sponsor, the Issuer or the Product, and nothing has happened that makes any Product Material untrue, inaccurate, incomplete or misleading.

2.8 No default, event of default, covenant breach, missed or deferred payment, suspension of redemptions or distributions, gating, restructuring or write-down of more than [PLACEHOLDER: percentage, suggested 10]% in the value of the Product or its assets has occurred, or, so far as the Sponsor is aware, is likely to occur.

### Offering basis

2.9 The Product may lawfully be offered, marketed and sold to the investor categories, in each Eligible Country, on the offering basis stated in the Product Listing Schedule, and that offering basis remains valid.

2.10 Every filing, notification, registration and consent required for that offering has been made or obtained and remains in force, and no limit on the number of offerees or investors in any Eligible Country has been, or will by reason of the Product's availability on the Platform be, exceeded.

2.11 The Eligible Countries, eligible investor categories and Selling Restrictions in the Product Listing Schedule are complete, accurate and current, so that the Platform can gate access to the Product correctly. The Product is not being offered to US persons through the Platform.

### Litigation, regulatory and sanctions matters

2.12 There is no litigation, arbitration, regulatory inquiry or investigation, enforcement action, sanction, fine, public censure, or suspension, variation or withdrawal of any licence pending or, so far as the Sponsor is aware, threatened against any member of the Sponsor Group, the Product, or any of their directors, controllers or key persons, and no material complaint about the Product is outstanding.

2.13 No member of the Sponsor Group, nor any of their directors, officers or controllers, nor any counterparty to the Product, is a Sanctioned Person, and neither the Product nor its assets, revenues or counterparties involve any Sanctioned Person or sanctioned country or territory.

2.14 No member of the Sponsor Group, nor any of their directors, officers, employees or agents, has engaged in bribery, corruption, money laundering, terrorist financing, tax evasion or the facilitation of tax evasion in connection with the Product.

2.15 No member of the Sponsor Group or the Issuer is insolvent, or subject to any insolvency, administration, receivership, liquidation or similar process.

### Funds flow

2.16 All subscription money for the Product is paid only to the payee and account set out in Part 6.5 of the Product Listing Schedule (the Sponsor, its administrator or an escrow agent), never to Multiplier, and is held and applied as described in the Offering Documents. Those payment instructions are accurate and current, and have not been changed other than through the Platform's controlled process.

2.17 [Where an escrow agent or administrator is used:] The escrow or administration arrangements described in the Product Listing Schedule are in force, and the escrow agent or administrator is not aware of any breach of them.

### Conflicts

2.18 All material conflicts of interest relating to the Product, including any interest of any member of the Sponsor Group or its key persons in the Product's assets, counterparties or service providers, and any fees or benefits they receive, are fully disclosed in the Offering Documents.

### Assets

2.19 [Where the Product is backed by physical or collectible assets:] The relevant member of the Sponsor Group or the Issuer has good title to (or the right to acquire) the assets described in the Offering Documents, and the custody, storage, insurance and valuation arrangements are in force and as described.

### Obligations to Multiplier

2.20 The Sponsor has complied with its obligations under SPN-02, including its obligations to report every Closing, to pay all Fees when due, and to notify Multiplier of the events in clause 16.1 of SPN-02.

2.21 No member of the Sponsor Group has paid, given, offered or agreed to pay or give any fee, commission or other benefit to any Platform Adviser in connection with any Platform Investor or any Funded Investment, other than through Multiplier.

## 4. Reliance and consequences

4.1 The Sponsor acknowledges that Multiplier relies on this certificate in making the Product available, and keeping it available, on the Platform, and that Multiplier has no duty to investigate or check it.

4.2 Each certification in this certificate is also a warranty given by the Sponsor under SPN-02.

4.3 If any certification is or becomes untrue, inaccurate or misleading, Multiplier may exercise its rights under SPN-02, including to suspend or remove the Product (clause 19), to claim under the indemnity (clause 25), and to terminate (clause 28). The Fees remain payable.
