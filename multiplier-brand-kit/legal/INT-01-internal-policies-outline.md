## 2. Conflicts of Interest Policy

**Owner:** [PLACEHOLDER: compliance lead]. **Adopted by:** [PLACEHOLDER: board].

2.1 **Identifying conflicts**
- Multiplier is paid by Sponsors (screening fees, listing fees and success fees), creating a potential incentive to list or promote Products.
- Multiplier shares Deal Fees with Adviser Firms (the Adviser Share), creating a potential incentive for advisers to favour Products listed on the Platform or with higher fees.
- Overrides for introducing advisers (paid only from the Multiplier Share) may create incentives to recruit rather than serve clients.
- Points and leaderboard schemes may incentivise volume over suitability.
- Staff personal investments, outside interests, and relationships with Sponsors or Adviser Firms.

2.2 **Fees from Sponsors**
- Screening decisions made independently of fee income; screening fee is non-refundable and payable whether or not a Product is listed.
- Success fee rate card applied consistently [PLACEHOLDER: whether rates vary by Product and how disclosed].
- Product presentation on the Platform is neutral; no paid placement or ranking [PLACEHOLDER: confirm, or describe and disclose any featured placements].
- Remuneration disclosed to Investors in the Investor Terms (MPL-INV-01).

2.3 **Adviser remuneration and Overrides**
- Adviser Share applied at a standard rate (70% of Deal Fees by default) [PLACEHOLDER: whether any Product-specific variation is permitted and how it is disclosed].
- Overrides paid only from the Multiplier Share, never reducing the Adviser Share or increasing the cost to Investors.
- Adviser Firms responsible for disclosing their remuneration to clients under their own rules; Multiplier provides the information needed to do so.
- Monitoring of referral patterns for misuse.

2.4 **Points and leaderboard**

- Secure software development lifecycle: code review, dependency scanning, separation of environments, no real personal data in test.
