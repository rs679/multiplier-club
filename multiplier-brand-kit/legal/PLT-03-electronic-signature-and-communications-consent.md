> Drafting note: The Platform is not offered to US persons, so US ESIGN Act disclosures are not strictly needed; this consent nonetheless follows that model because it is widely recognised. Counsel should confirm, for each launch country, which documents can be signed by simple electronic signature, which need an advanced or qualified electronic signature, and which (for example powers of attorney, notarised or apostilled forms, or certain trust documents) cannot be signed electronically at all.

4.1.1 the document ID, title and version;

4.1.2 a cryptographic hash of the document text as presented to you;

4.1.3 your name, user ID, role and, where relevant, your firm;

4.1.4 the date and time (in UTC) of each step, including when the document was presented, opened and signed;

4.1.5 your IP address, and browser and device information;
