## 3. How signatures are captured

3.1 Depending on the document and the law that applies, you may accept or sign a Platform Document by:

3.1.1 **click-through:** clicking a button such as "I agree" or "Accept";

3.1.2 **checkbox:** ticking one or more boxes confirming specific statements, followed by a confirmation click;

3.1.3 **typed name:** typing your full name in a signature field;

3.1.4 **drawn signature:** drawing your signature with a mouse, trackpad, stylus or finger;

3.1.5 **provider e-signature:** signing through our electronic signature provider, which may involve email verification, a one-time code sent by SMS, or similar authentication; or

3.1.6 **advanced or qualified electronic signature:** where the law of a particular country or a counterparty requires it, signing with an advanced or qualified electronic signature (for example using a certificate-based signature or an identity-verified signing flow).

3.2 The Platform will show you, at the point of signing, which method applies to the document and what you are agreeing to.

3.3 Some firm-level documents (for example the Adviser Firm Agreement and the Sponsor Listing Agreement) must be accepted or signed by a Firm Administrator or other authorised signatory, and take effect only once Multiplier has also accepted them. Multiplier accepts the Adviser Firm Agreement by enabling the adviser's access on the Platform, and accepts the Sponsor Listing Agreement by countersigning it, unless the document says otherwise.

3.4 You must not allow anyone else to sign on your behalf using your Account. Any signature made using your Account is treated as yours, subject to clause 5 of the Platform Terms of Use.

3.5 If a document cannot lawfully be signed electronically in your country, we will tell you and arrange another method.

## 4. Audit trail and records

4.1 For each acceptance or signature, we record:

4.1.1 the document ID, title and version;

4.1.2 a cryptographic hash of the document text as presented to you;

4.1.3 your name, user ID, role and, where relevant, your firm;

4.1.4 the date and time (in UTC) of each step, including when the document was presented, opened and signed;

4.1.5 your IP address, and browser and device information;

4.1.6 the signature method used and any authentication steps completed (for example a one-time code); and

4.1.7 for provider e-signatures, the provider's own certificate of completion or audit log.

4.2 We keep a copy of each signed or accepted document together with its audit record, for the retention period set out in our Platform Privacy Notice.

4.3 You agree that these records are admissible as evidence of the document, its content and your acceptance or signature, to the extent the law allows.

4.4 You can view the documents you have accepted or signed on the Platform at any time while you have an Account. Documents are viewed on the Platform only. We do not currently offer download or printing. You can ask us for a copy under clause 5.
