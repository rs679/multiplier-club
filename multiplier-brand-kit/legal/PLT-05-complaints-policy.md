## 2. What counts as a complaint

2.1 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, spoken or written, about the Platform or our service, where you are looking for a response or a remedy. You do not need to use the word "complaint".

2.2 Anyone who uses the Platform, or has been affected by it, can complain. Someone else (for example a family member, adviser or lawyer) can complain on your behalf if you authorise them in writing.

## 3. Who deals with your complaint

3.1 Several parties are involved when you use the Platform, and each is responsible for its own part. Your complaint will be dealt with by the party responsible for the issue:

| What the complaint is about | Who deals with it | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **A Product** | The **Sponsor** of the Product (or its administrator) | Product performance, valuations, distributions, capital calls, Product documents, the Sponsor's decision to accept or decline a subscription, investor reporting |
| **Advice** | The **Adviser Firm** that advised you | Whether a Product was suitable or appropriate for you, what your adviser told you, your adviser's fees or conduct |
| **The Platform or our service** | **Multiplier** | Account access, technical problems, how we handled your data, how we gated access, our communications, our customer service, adviser fee payments (for Adviser Firms), screening and listing processes (for Sponsors) |
