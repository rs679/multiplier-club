## Why we ask for this (shown to the investor)

Before you can invest in a Product, the Sponsor (or its administrator) must check who you are, where your money comes from, and your tax status. The law requires this.

To save you time, we collect this information once and share it with the Sponsors you choose. Each Sponsor makes its own decision on whether to accept you, and may ask you for more information.

Please answer accurately. If you are completing this form for a company, trust, family office or fund, the questions refer to that entity, and there are extra questions about its owners and controllers.
