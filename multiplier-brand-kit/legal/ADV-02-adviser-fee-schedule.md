## 1. Default fee split

1.1 Unless a Product Fee Line states otherwise, Deal Fees are split as follows:

| Share | Percentage of Deal Fees | Paid to |
|---|---|---|
| **Adviser Share** | **70%** | The Adviser Firm whose Introduced Investor made the Funded Investment |
| **Multiplier Share** | **30%** | Multiplier. Any Overrides under the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03) are paid only out of this share. |

1.2 The Adviser Share is paid when paid: only when, and to the extent that, Multiplier receives the relevant success fee from the Sponsor in cleared funds (MPL-ADV-01 clause 11.3).

1.3 Overrides never reduce the Adviser Share of any Adviser Firm.

## 2. What counts as Deal Fees

2.1 **Deal Fees** are success fees actually received in cleared funds by Multiplier from a Sponsor in respect of Funded Investments made by your Introduced Investors, net of VAT and other taxes, refunds, credits and chargebacks (MPL-ADV-01 clause 1.1.12).

2.2 In summary:

| Included in Deal Fees | Not included in Deal Fees |

| Success fees on Funded Investments by your Introduced Investors, including on later closings, top-ups and further drawdowns where the Product's fee terms charge a success fee on them | Screening fees and listing fees paid by Sponsors [OPTION A: excluded (recommended) / OPTION B: listing fees included as set out in MPL-ADV-01 clause 1.1.12, Option B] |
| | Any success fee that the Sponsor has not paid, or has paid and later reclaimed |
| | VAT and other taxes, and amounts withheld by the Sponsor |
| | Fees on investments by Investors who are not attributed to you (MPL-ADV-01 clause 10) |
| | Interest or late-payment charges received from Sponsors [OPTION: unless this Schedule says otherwise] |

## 3. Product-specific variations and the Product Fee Line

3.1 Some Products have different fee terms, for example a different success-fee rate, a different Adviser Share, a success fee charged on drawdowns rather than on commitment, or a different holdback or clawback period. These are shown in the **Product Fee Line**.

3.2 **Before each share.** Before an Authorised User shares a Product with a client, invites a client to it, or requests access to it for a client, the Platform shows the Product Fee Line and asks the Authorised User to confirm it. The share cannot proceed until it is confirmed.

3.3 **Fee terms locked at share.** The Adviser Share percentage, holdback and clawback period shown in the Product Fee Line at the time of the first share with an Introduced Investor apply to all Funded Investments by that Investor in that Product resulting from that share (MPL-ADV-01 clause 11.2), even if the Product Fee Line later changes. The success fee itself is whatever the Sponsor actually pays Multiplier.

## 9. Overrides

9.1 If you introduce other adviser firms under the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03), you may earn an Override on Deal Fees generated by the firms you introduced directly. The rate depends on your Override Tier. Tiers are alternatives and do not add together. Overrides are a percentage of Deal Fees and are paid only out of the Multiplier Share.

| Override Tier | Override rate (percentage of Deal Fees) |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 3% |
| Tier 2 | 5% |
| Tier 3 | 10% |
| Maximum Override on any Deal Fee | 10% of Deal Fees |
| Minimum Multiplier retention after the Override | 20% of Deal Fees |

9.2 Overrides are paid when paid, on the same timetable as the Adviser Share, and are subject to the same holdback and clawback.

## 10. Waiver election

The election applies from the next share after it is made. The Product Fee Line will show the effect of the election for that Product.

## 11. Worked examples

These examples are illustrative only. They assume no VAT is deducted from the success fee, no refunds, the default 70/30 split and a payment in the same currency. Where an example uses a percentage that is still a placeholder in this Schedule or in MPL-ADV-03, the figure used is labelled "illustrative" and is not the programme rate.

### Example A: basic split

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Funded Investment by your Introduced Investor | | USD 1,000,000 |
| Success fee paid by the Sponsor to Multiplier (2.5%) | 1,000,000 x 2.5% | USD 25,000 |
| Deal Fees | Received in cleared funds, net of VAT and refunds | USD 25,000 |
| **Adviser Share (70%)** | 25,000 x 70% | **USD 17,500** |
| **Multiplier Share (30%)**, before Overrides | 25,000 x 30% | **USD 7,500** |

The Adviser Share of USD 17,500 is paid within [X] Business Days of Multiplier receiving the USD 25,000 in cleared funds.

### Example B: with holdback

Using an illustrative holdback of 10% for [X] months:

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Adviser Share | | USD 17,500 |
| Holdback (illustrative 10%) | 17,500 x 10% | USD 1,750 |
| Paid now | 17,500 - 1,750 | USD 15,750 |
| Released after [X] months, if no Clawback Event | | USD 1,750 |

Formula: amount paid now = Adviser Share x (100% - holdback [X]%).

### Example C: an Override paid out of the Multiplier Share

Suppose your firm was introduced to the Platform by Firm R, and Firm R is in Override Tier 2 (5%):

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Deal Fees | | USD 25,000 |
| Your Adviser Share (unchanged) | 25,000 x 70% | USD 17,500 |
| Multiplier Share before the Override | 25,000 x 30% | USD 7,500 |
| Override to Firm R (Tier 2, 5%) | 25,000 x 5% | USD 1,250 |
| Multiplier retains | 7,500 - 1,250 (25% of Deal Fees) | USD 6,250 |

Formulas: Override = Deal Fees x the Referring Adviser's tier rate. Only one Override is paid on any Deal Fee. It may not exceed 10% of Deal Fees, and Multiplier must retain at least 20% of Deal Fees. If either limit would be breached, the Override is reduced as set out in MPL-ADV-03 clause 5.5. If Firm R were in Tier 3 (10%), the Override would be USD 2,500 and Multiplier would retain USD 5,000 (20%).

### Example D: clawback on early redemption

The Investor in Example A redeems half of the investment [X] months after the Funded Investment, within the redemption clawback period. Under Option A (clawback in full):

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Adviser Share paid on the redeemed half | 17,500 x 50% | USD 8,750 |
| Recovered from holdback (Example B) | | USD 1,750 |
| Recovered by set-off against your next payments, or repaid within 30 days of demand | 8,750 - 1,750 | USD 7,000 |

Any Overrides paid on the same Deal Fees are clawed back from the firms that received them under MPL-ADV-03.
