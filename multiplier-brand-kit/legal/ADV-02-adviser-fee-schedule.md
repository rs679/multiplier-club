## 1. Default fee split

1.1 Unless a Product Fee Line states otherwise, Deal Fees are split as follows:

| Share | Percentage of Deal Fees | Paid to |
|---|---|---|
| **Adviser Share** | **70%** | The Adviser Firm whose Introduced Investor made the Funded Investment |
| **Multiplier Share** | **30%** | Multiplier. Any Overrides under the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03) are paid only out of this share. |

1.2 The Adviser Share is paid when paid: only when, and to the extent that, Multiplier receives the relevant success fee from the Sponsor in cleared funds (MPL-ADV-01 clause 11.3).

1.3 Overrides never reduce the Adviser Share of any Adviser Firm.

## 2. What counts as Deal Fees

2.1 **Deal Fees** are success fees actually received in cleared funds by Multiplier from a Sponsor in respect of Funded Investments made by your Introduced Investors, net of VAT and other taxes, refunds, credits and chargebacks (MPL-ADV-01 clause 1.1.12).

2.2 In summary:

| Included in Deal Fees | Not included in Deal Fees |
|---|---|
| Success fees on Funded Investments by your Introduced Investors, including on later closings, top-ups and further drawdowns where the Product's fee terms charge a success fee on them | Screening fees and listing fees paid by Sponsors |
| | Any success fee that the Sponsor has not paid, or has paid and later reclaimed |
| | VAT and other taxes, and amounts withheld by the Sponsor |
| | Fees on investments by Investors who are not attributed to you (MPL-ADV-01 clause 10) |
| | Interest or late-payment charges received from Sponsors |

## 3. Product-specific variations and the Product Fee Line

3.1 Some Products have different fee terms, for example a different success-fee rate, a different Adviser Share, a success fee charged on drawdowns rather than on commitment, or a different holdback or clawback period. These are shown in the **Product Fee Line**.

3.2 **Before each share.** Before an Authorised User shares a Product with a client, invites a client to it, or requests access to it for a client, the Platform shows the Product Fee Line and asks the Authorised User to confirm it. The share cannot proceed until it is confirmed.

3.3 **Fee terms locked at share.** The Adviser Share percentage, holdback and clawback period shown in the Product Fee Line at the time of the first share with an Introduced Investor apply to all Funded Investments by that Investor in that Product resulting from that share (MPL-ADV-01 clause 11.2), even if the Product Fee Line later changes. The success fee itself is whatever the Sponsor actually pays Multiplier.

3.4 **Form of the Product Fee Line.** The Product Fee Line shows the Product, the Fee Schedule and Product fee terms versions, the success fee and its basis, your Adviser Share, when it is paid, any holdback and clawback period, and any other Product-specific terms. As an illustration only, for a Product with a success fee of 2.5% of the amount funded and the default fee split, it would read:

> **Fee line: Example Product** (Fee Schedule version 0.9, Product fee terms version 1)
> - Success fee paid by the Sponsor to Multiplier: 2.5% of the amount funded by Platform Investors.
> - Your Adviser Share: 70% of Deal Fees. Estimated Adviser Share per USD 100,000 funded: USD 1,750, before VAT, holdback and any clawback.
> - Paid when paid: within 10 Business Days of Multiplier receiving the success fee in cleared funds, or monthly in arrears with your statement.
> - Holdback: none. Clawback: if the client redeems within 12 months, and in the other cases in MPL-ADV-01 clause 13.
> - You must disclose your remuneration to your client as your rules require.
>
> Confirmation: "I confirm these fee terms apply to investments resulting from this share."

3.5 **Product-specific table.** Multiplier maintains the following table on the Platform. Each row is shown as a Product Fee Line.

| Product | Sponsor | Success-fee rate and basis | Adviser Share | Multiplier Share | Holdback | Redemption clawback period | Other terms | Fee terms version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Each Product listed on the Platform | As shown in the Product Fee Line | As shown in the Product Fee Line | 70% unless the Product Fee Line states otherwise | 30% unless the Product Fee Line states otherwise | None unless the Product Fee Line states otherwise | 12 months unless the Product Fee Line states otherwise | As shown in the Product Fee Line | As shown in the Product Fee Line |

> Drafting note: Where the Adviser Share for a Product is above 70%, the Multiplier Share falls and the Override Cap and Minimum Retention in the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03 clauses 5.4 and 5.5) will reduce or remove Overrides on that Product. The Platform should flag this when a Product's fee terms are set.

## 5. Holdback

5.1 No holdback applies by default.

5.2 A Product Fee Line may set a holdback for a Product, for example where the Product has an early-redemption right or a longer cooling-off period.

5.3 Holdback is released automatically at the end of the period, less any amounts set off for Clawback Events. No interest is paid on holdback.

## 6. Clawback periods

6.1 The Clawback Events are set out in MPL-ADV-01 clause 13.3. The periods during which each may be relied on are:

| Clawback Event | MPL-ADV-01 clause | Period | Amount clawed back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor refunds or reclaims the success fee | 13.3.1 | For as long as the Sponsor may require repayment under its agreement with Multiplier | Adviser Share on the fee refunded or reclaimed |
| Subscription cancelled, rescinded or unwound | 13.3.2 | 12 months after the Funded Investment, or longer if the cancellation right lasts longer | Adviser Share on the cancelled part |
| Investor redeems or exits early | 13.3.3 | 12 months after the Funded Investment, unless the Product Fee Line states another period | Adviser Share on the redeemed part, in full |
| Misconduct, misrepresentation or breach | 13.3.4 | Six years from the date of the Funded Investment | Whole Adviser Share on the affected Funded Investment, plus Multiplier's losses |
| Regulatory finding or upheld complaint linked to the sale | 13.3.5 | Six years from the date of the Funded Investment | Whole Adviser Share on the affected Funded Investment, plus Multiplier's losses |

6.2 Clawback is recovered first from holdback and future payments by set-off, and otherwise must be repaid within 30 days of demand. Clawback rights survive the end of the Adviser Firm Agreement.

## 7. Currency and FX

7.1 Multiplier pays the Adviser Share in your payout currency. Your payout currency is US dollars (USD) unless Multiplier has agreed another currency that it supports with you, as recorded on the Platform.

7.2 Where Deal Fees are received in a different currency, Multiplier converts them at the rate obtained by Multiplier or its payment provider on or around the date of payment to you. Conversion costs and bank charges are borne by you (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.5).

7.3 Statements show the original currency amount, the rate used and the converted amount.

## 8. Invoicing and tax

8.1 You either invoice Multiplier for each payment, or Multiplier issues self-billed invoices on your behalf under the Self-Billing Agreement (MPL-ADV-06), where available in your jurisdiction (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.6).

8.2 The Adviser Share is stated exclusive of VAT. If VAT is chargeable on your supply to Multiplier, Multiplier pays it in addition on receipt of a valid VAT invoice or self-billed invoice, unless the reverse charge or an exemption applies (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.7).

8.3 Multiplier deducts any tax it is required by law to withhold and gives you evidence of payment. Payments are not grossed up (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.9).

## 9. Overrides

9.1 The Referral Programme is open to individual advisers only. If you are an individual adviser acting alone and you introduce other individual advisers under the Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03), you may earn an Override on Deal Fees generated by the advisers you introduced directly. Firms do not earn or generate Overrides. The rate depends on your Override Tier. Tiers are alternatives and do not add together. Overrides are a percentage of Deal Fees and are paid only out of the Multiplier Share.

| Override Tier | Override rate (percentage of Deal Fees) |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 3% |
| Tier 2 | 5% |
| Tier 3 | 10% |
| Maximum Override on any Deal Fee | 10% of Deal Fees |
| Minimum Multiplier retention after the Override | 20% of Deal Fees |

9.2 Overrides are paid when paid, on the same timetable as the Adviser Share, and are subject to the same holdback and clawback.

## 10. Waiver election

10.1 If your rules prevent you from receiving third-party payments for some clients or Products, you may elect on the Platform not to receive the Adviser Share for them (MPL-ADV-01 clause 11.6). The election applies from the next share after it is made. The Product Fee Line will show the effect of the election for that Product.

## 11. Worked examples

These examples are illustrative only. They assume no VAT is deducted from the success fee, no refunds, the default 70/30 split and a payment in the same currency. Where an example uses a figure that is not fixed by this Schedule (such as the success-fee rate or a holdback), the figure is labelled "illustrative" and is not a rate that applies to any Product.

### Example A: basic split

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Funded Investment by your Introduced Investor | | USD 1,000,000 |
| Success fee paid by the Sponsor to Multiplier (illustrative 2.5%) | 1,000,000 x 2.5% | USD 25,000 |
| Deal Fees | Received in cleared funds, net of VAT and refunds | USD 25,000 |
| **Adviser Share (70%)** | 25,000 x 70% | **USD 17,500** |
| **Multiplier Share (30%)**, before Overrides | 25,000 x 30% | **USD 7,500** |

The Adviser Share of USD 17,500 is paid within 10 Business Days of Multiplier receiving the USD 25,000 in cleared funds, or with the monthly statement.

### Example B: with holdback

No holdback applies by default. This example assumes that a Product Fee Line sets an illustrative holdback of 10% for 6 months:

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Adviser Share | | USD 17,500 |
| Holdback (illustrative 10%) | 17,500 x 10% | USD 1,750 |
| Paid now | 17,500 - 1,750 | USD 15,750 |
| Released after 6 months, if no Clawback Event | | USD 1,750 |

Formula: amount paid now = Adviser Share x (100% - holdback percentage).

### Example C: an Override paid out of the Multiplier Share

Suppose you are an individual adviser who was introduced to the Platform by Adviser R, another individual adviser, and Adviser R is in Override Tier 2 (5%):

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Deal Fees | | USD 25,000 |
| Your Adviser Share (unchanged) | 25,000 x 70% | USD 17,500 |
| Multiplier Share before the Override | 25,000 x 30% | USD 7,500 |
| Override to Adviser R (Tier 2, 5%) | 25,000 x 5% | USD 1,250 |
| Multiplier retains | 7,500 - 1,250 (25% of Deal Fees) | USD 6,250 |

Formulas: Override = Deal Fees x the Referring Adviser's tier rate. Only one Override is paid on any Deal Fee. It may not exceed 10% of Deal Fees, and Multiplier must retain at least 20% of Deal Fees. If either limit would be breached, the Override is reduced as set out in MPL-ADV-03 clause 5.5. If Adviser R were in Tier 3 (10%), the Override would be USD 2,500 and Multiplier would retain USD 5,000 (20%).

### Example D: clawback on early redemption

The Investor in Example A redeems half of the investment 4 months after the Funded Investment, within the 12-month redemption clawback period and while the illustrative holdback in Example B is still held. The Adviser Share on the redeemed part is clawed back in full:

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Adviser Share paid on the redeemed half | 17,500 x 50% | USD 8,750 |
| Recovered from holdback (Example B) | | USD 1,750 |
| Recovered by set-off against your next payments, or repaid within 30 days of demand | 8,750 - 1,750 | USD 7,000 |

Any Overrides paid on the same Deal Fees are clawed back from the advisers that received them under MPL-ADV-03.
