## 2. Core principles

2.1 **Fair, clear and not misleading.** Every communication must be fair, clear and not misleading, balanced, and appropriate for the person receiving it.

2.2 **Neutral outside, detail inside.** Outside the Platform, communications are neutral: who is inviting, what Multiplier is, and how to register. Product terms, returns, fees, performance and documents stay inside the Platform, behind registration, eligibility confirmation and the Product's distribution restrictions.

2.2A **Headline cards only before gating.** Inside the Platform, a user who has not completed a Product's gating sees only its anonymised headline card (reference code, asset class, broad sector, broad region, type of instrument, currency and status). Nobody may add to a headline card, or say anything alongside it, that names or could identify the Product, the Issuer, the Sponsor or the underlying investment. This includes sharing a headline card with a client: share the card as it is, with no added detail.

2.3 **The documents are the source.** Product information comes only from the Sponsor's current Offering Documents on the Platform. Nobody adds to, improves on or summarises them in a way that changes their meaning.

2.4 **Everyone owns their own words.** Sponsors are responsible for their Product Materials. Advisers are responsible for their communications with their clients, and for any advice or recommendation they give. Multiplier does not sign off any User's communications, and its review of content on the Platform (for format and these Guidelines) is not a sign-off of accuracy or legality.

2.5 **No US persons.** The Platform is not available to US persons. Do not invite or market to them.

## 3. How to describe Multiplier

3.1 Multiplier may be described only as a technology platform. The fixed description is:

> Multiplier is a technology platform. It does not give investment, legal or tax advice, does not make personal recommendations, does not hold or receive investor money, and does not underwrite, recommend or endorse any Product.

3.2 **Screening wording.** Multiplier's screening may be described only in these exact words, with no addition, shortening or paraphrase:

> "We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product."

3.3 Do not say or imply that:

3.3.1 Multiplier has reviewed, checked, selected, rated or endorsed any Product, or that a Product is "on Multiplier" because it is good;

3.3.2 Multiplier is your partner, employer, principal, agent or joint venturer (Adviser Firms and Sponsors are independent of Multiplier);

3.3.3 Multiplier is regulated, authorised or supervised by any authority, except in the exact regulatory status wording Multiplier publishes for the relevant country ([PLACEHOLDER: Multiplier regulatory status statements]); or

3.3.4 Multiplier holds, protects or is responsible for investors' money (subscription money goes directly to the Sponsor, its administrator or an escrow agent, never to Multiplier).

## 4. Words and claims that must never be used

4.1 Never use the following words or phrases about Multiplier, the Platform, any Sponsor or any Product, in any language:

| Do not use | Why | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| "vetted" | Implies Multiplier has assessed the merits of the Product | Use the screening wording in 3.2 |
| "verified deal" or "verified" about a Product or Sponsor | "Verified" refers only to a person's identity or status | Say "available on Multiplier" |
| "approved" | Implies endorsement by Multiplier or a regulator | Describe the factual status, for example "open for subscriptions" |
| "guaranteed" | Capital and returns in private markets are at risk | State the terms exactly as in the Offering Documents, with risk wording |
| "safe", "secure", "risk-free", "low risk", "can't lose", "protected" | All Products carry risk, including loss of capital | Use the standard risk wording (Annex B) |
| "best" (for example "best deal", "best returns") | Unsubstantiated superlative | Describe objectively, or not at all |
| "AI due diligence" | Multiplier does not carry out due diligence on Products for investors | Do not describe any tool as due diligence |
| "exclusive", "limited time", "act now", "only a few places left" | Creates undue pressure | State factual closing dates only inside the Platform |
| "endorsed by", "recommended by", "selected by", "backed by" Multiplier | Multiplier does not endorse, recommend or back any Product | Use the fixed description in 3.1 |

4.2 Also do not:

4.2.1 promise or imply any return, income, yield, capital growth or exit;

4.2.2 compare a Product with bank deposits, savings accounts or government bonds in a way that suggests similar security or liquidity;

4.2.3 suggest a Product is liquid, or can be sold or redeemed at any time, unless the Offering Documents say so and the conditions are stated;

4.2.4 make tax claims, other than as stated in the Offering Documents and with a statement that tax treatment depends on individual circumstances and may change;

4.2.5 use testimonials, investor case studies or endorsements about any Product; or

4.2.6 use leaderboard positions, points, badges or rankings under the Points and Leaderboard Rules (ADV-04) in any communication to investors, or as a statement about any Product.

## 5. Invitations to the Platform

5.1 An invitation sent outside the Platform (by email, link, QR code, message or in person) may contain only:

5.1.1 the name of the person and firm sending it, and the firm's regulatory status statement where its rules require one;

5.1.2 a short description of Multiplier, using the fixed description in 3.1;

5.1.3 how to register (the Platform-generated link or QR code); and

5.1.4 a way to opt out of further messages, where the channel or law requires one.

5.2 An invitation must not contain any Product terms, returns, yields, targets, fees, performance, minimums, closing dates, documents, images of assets, or any statement about the merits of any Product or Sponsor.

5.3 [OPTION A: An invitation must not name any Product or Sponsor. / OPTION B: An invitation may name a Product, its Sponsor and its asset class (and nothing else about it) only where the recipient is an existing client of the Adviser Firm and the Adviser Firm's compliance rules allow it.]

5.4 Use the templates in Annex A, or the templates built into the Platform. If you change them, the result must still comply with this clause 5.

5.5 Invite only people you reasonably believe may be eligible to use the Platform in their country and who are not US persons. Do not use bought, rented or scraped contact lists.

## 6. Product information, returns and performance

6.1 Product information may be shared only inside the Platform, with people who have passed the Product's gating, or, for an Adviser Firm, with its own client who has passed that gating, as part of the adviser's regulated service and under its own rules.

6.2 Return, yield, target, income and performance figures may be used only:

6.2.1 exactly as stated in the current Offering Documents on the Platform;

6.2.2 with their basis stated (for example target or actual, gross or net of fees, annualised or cumulative, the period covered, and whether audited);

6.2.3 with equal prominence to the required risk wording in Annex B and any Product-specific risk wording the Sponsor provides; and

6.2.4 where past performance is mentioned, with the statement that past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

6.3 Do not create your own forecasts, illustrations, scenarios, charts or comparisons for a Product. Do not quote figures selectively or out of context.

6.4 **Sponsors.** All Product Materials must be signed off by an appropriately authorised person within the Sponsor Group (or an external authorised person) where the law requires it, before upload. Fees, costs and conflicts must be stated as clearly as returns.

6.5 **Advisers.** Any personal recommendation is the adviser's own and must meet the adviser's own suitability or appropriateness rules. Do not present Platform content as Multiplier's view or advice.

6.6 **Fee disclosure.** Do not hide or play down the fact that Multiplier receives fees from Sponsors and shares them with Adviser Firms. Disclose remuneration as your rules require.

## 7. Permitted channels

7.1 The following channels may be used for invitations and for communications about the Platform, on the conditions stated. Any channel not listed is not permitted without Multiplier's prior written consent.

| Channel | Permitted | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Yes | From the Platform, or from the User's own business email. Neutral content only (clause 5). Include an opt-out where required. |
| Tracked links | Yes | Platform-generated links only. Do not alter, shorten through a third-party service or remove tracking. Send to identified individuals; do not post publicly. |
| QR codes | Yes | Platform-generated QR codes only, used in one-to-one or private settings (for example a printed card handed to a client or a private event). Not in public advertising. |
| WhatsApp | Only where the User's firm permits it | Click-to-chat from the User's own number with the Platform's pre-filled neutral message. Only to people who have agreed to be contacted that way. No documents, images of assets, screenshots or Product details. Not available where the firm uses "bank mode". |
| SMS | Only where the User's firm permits it | Same conditions as WhatsApp. |
| Phone and in person | Yes | Neutral description of Multiplier only; Product discussions only with a client who has passed the Product's gating, under the adviser's own rules. No unsolicited calls where the law prohibits them. |
| Social media and public websites | Limited | General statements about Multiplier only, using the fixed description in 3.1, with the User's firm compliance sign-off. No Product names, terms, returns, images, invite links or QR codes. |
| Paid advertising, mass messaging, bulk email | No | Not permitted without Multiplier's prior written consent. |

7.2 Bank mode: where an Adviser Firm has enabled "bank mode" on the Platform, only email and tracked links are available, and the firm's users must not use WhatsApp, SMS or other personal channels to communicate about the Platform or any Product.

## 8. Data rooms and documents

8.1 Documents in a Product's data room must stay on the Platform. Do not download (unless the Platform offers a download for the User's own use), forward, email, print for distribution, screenshot, photograph, copy, upload to any other system (including any AI tool or shared drive), or otherwise share them outside the Platform.

8.2 An adviser may discuss data-room documents with a client who has access to the same data room. To share a document with a client, invite the client to the Product through the Platform.

8.3 Do not remove or obscure any watermark, version marking or legend.

8.4 Everyone who opens a data room is bound by the Data Room Confidentiality Undertaking (INV-04).

## 9. Images, logos and names

9.1 **Multiplier's brand.** Use Multiplier's name and logo only in the form supplied in Multiplier's brand kit and in line with its brand guidelines (the logo is only ever solid white or solid black; do not recolour, distort, animate or combine it with other marks). Use them only to identify Multiplier as the platform you use, never to suggest endorsement or partnership. The brand name is "Multiplier".

9.2 **Sponsor logos and names.** Sponsors license their names and logos to Multiplier under SPN-02 for use on the Platform. Adviser Firms must not use a Sponsor's name or logo outside the Platform without that Sponsor's written consent.

9.3 **Images of assets.** Images of a Product's assets (for example a property, a whisky cask or warehouse, artwork or a development site) must be genuine and current, or clearly labelled "illustrative". They may be shown only inside the Platform. Do not use images that suggest wealth, security or certainty of return.

9.4 **Third-party marks.** Do not use the logo or name of any regulator, government body, exchange, bank, auditor or other third party in a way that suggests it endorses or supervises any Product or Multiplier. Never use a regulator's logo.

9.5 **People.** Do not use images of real people (including clients or staff) without their written consent, and never in a way that suggests a testimonial about a Product.
