## 2. Structure of this Agreement

2.1 This Agreement is a master agreement. Each Product is listed under its own Product Listing Schedule, which forms part of this Agreement. A Product Listing Schedule takes effect only when signed by the Sponsor (and, where the Schedule requires, the Issuer) and countersigned by Multiplier.

2.2 If there is a conflict between documents, the following order of priority applies: (a) the clauses of this Agreement; (b) the Schedules to this Agreement; (c) the Product Listing Schedule; (d) the Content and Marketing Guidelines (PLT-06). A "special term" in a Product Listing Schedule takes priority over a clause of this Agreement only for that Product, and only if it expressly names the clause it overrides.

2.3 Where an Issuer signs a Product Listing Schedule, the Sponsor and the Issuer are jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this Agreement relating to that Product. Where an Issuer does not sign, the Sponsor must procure that the Issuer complies with this Agreement as if it were a party.

2.4 The Sponsor must procure that each member of the Sponsor Group, and each of their officers, employees, agents and Authorised Users, complies with the obligations in this Agreement that are expressed to apply to them, and is responsible for their acts and omissions as if they were its own.

## 4. The Services

4.1.1 **Listing:** (a) an anonymised headline card, visible to registered users who are not excluded by the Product's distribution restrictions, showing only the fields in Part 2A of the Product Listing Schedule and nothing that names or could reasonably identify the Product, the Issuer, the Sponsor or the underlying investment; and (b) a Product page, including a deal card and key-terms table in Multiplier's standard format, visible only to users who satisfy the Product's gating;

4.1.7 **Records:** records of access, acceptances and subscriptions relating to the Product.

## 7. Prior relationships and investments processed through the Platform

7.1 The Success Fee is payable on every Funded Investment that is Processed through the Platform, **regardless of any prior, existing or later relationship** between the Investor (or any Connected Person) and any member of the Sponsor Group. In particular, it makes no difference that the Investor:

7.1.1 was known to, had dealt with or had invested with any member of the Sponsor Group before;

7.1.2 is an existing investor in the Product or any other Sponsor Product;

7.1.3 was also introduced by, or claims to have been introduced by, another person;

7.1.4 first contacted, or was first contacted by, the Sponsor; or

7.1.5 would, in the Sponsor's view, have invested anyway.

7.2 A Funded Investment is **Processed through the Platform** if, before its Closing, the Investor or any of its Connected Persons (or a Platform Adviser on its behalf) did any of the following in relation to the Sponsor Product concerned: viewed it, requested access to it, indicated interest in it, opened its data room, asked a question about it, or started, completed or submitted any part of its subscription workflow, on or through the Platform; or if the Investor was invited to or introduced to that Sponsor Product by a Platform Adviser.

7.3 The Existing Investor List (if clause 9 applies) does not apply to any Funded Investment that is Processed through the Platform.

7.4 Where the Sponsor knows or ought reasonably to know that an Investor is a Platform Investor, it must not ask, encourage or allow that Investor to subscribe outside the Platform. If any such Investor subscribes outside the Platform, the Success Fee is payable as if the subscription had been Processed through the Platform, and the Sponsor must report it under clause 13.

7.5 If two or more intermediaries claim to have introduced the same Investor, any payment the Sponsor makes to another intermediary is a matter for the Sponsor, and Multiplier's Fees are not reduced (clause 6.11).
