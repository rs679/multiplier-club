# Self-Billing Agreement

> Drafting note: Self-billing rules differ by jurisdiction. Some tax authorities require prior notification or authorisation, specific wording on each invoice, a fixed review period (often 12 months), or a separate invoice number series for each supplier. Tax counsel should confirm, for each jurisdiction where self-billing is offered, the conditions, the required invoice wording and the VAT treatment of the Adviser Share and Overrides (which may be exempt as intermediation in financial services, standard rated, or subject to the reverse charge). Where self-billing is not available, the Adviser Firm invoices Multiplier under MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.6.1.

## Background

A. Under the Adviser Firm Agreement (MPL-ADV-01) and, where applicable, the Adviser Referral Programme Rules (MPL-ADV-03), you supply introduction and distribution services to Multiplier in connection with the Multiplier platform at app.multiplier.club and any related websites, apps and services (the **Platform**), in return for the Adviser Share and any Overrides.

B. To simplify payment, you agree that Multiplier will issue invoices for those supplies on your behalf (self-billing), on the terms of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

1.1.1 **Self-Billed Invoice** means an invoice, or a credit note, issued by Multiplier on your behalf under this Agreement;

1.1.2 **Supplies** means the services you supply to Multiplier under the Adviser Firm Agreement and the Referral Programme Rules for which the Adviser Share or an Override is payable;

1.1.3 **Tax Authority** means the tax authority named in the Agreement details, and any other authority responsible for VAT in respect of the Supplies; and

1.1.4 other words in capitals have the meanings given in the Adviser Firm Agreement.

## 2. What Multiplier agrees to do

2.1 **Issue invoices.** Multiplier will issue a Self-Billed Invoice for all Supplies made by you during the term of this Agreement, at the same time as the remuneration statement for the relevant payment (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.1) or within [PLACEHOLDER: number] days of the date of supply, whichever is earlier, or as local rules require.

2.2 **Content.** Each Self-Billed Invoice will include:

2.2.1 your name, address and VAT registration number (if any);

2.2.2 Multiplier's name, address and VAT registration number;

2.2.3 a unique, sequential invoice number from the invoice number series allocated to you;

2.2.4 the date of issue and the date of supply (tax point);

2.2.5 a description of the Supplies, including the statement period and the remuneration statement reference;

2.2.6 the amount payable excluding VAT, the VAT rate and the amount of VAT (or the reason no VAT is charged, such as an exemption or the reverse charge), and the total amount payable, in the invoice currency;

2.2.7 the statement: "[PLACEHOLDER: self-billing wording required by the Tax Authority, e.g. 'Self-billing invoice issued by the customer on behalf of the supplier. The VAT shown is your output tax due to [Tax Authority]']"; and

2.2.8 any other information required by the Tax Authority.

2.3 **Copies.** Multiplier will make a copy of each Self-Billed Invoice available to you on the Platform, and by email if you ask, at the time it is issued.

2.4 **Credit notes.** Where an amount is reduced or reclaimed (for example, through a Clawback Event, holdback adjustment or correction), Multiplier will issue a self-billed credit note referring to the original Self-Billed Invoice.

## 3. What you agree to do

3.1 **Accept Self-Billed Invoices.** You agree to accept each Self-Billed Invoice issued by Multiplier for Supplies made by you during the term of this Agreement.

3.2 **No other invoices.** You must not issue your own sales invoices for any Supplies covered by this Agreement. If you do, Multiplier may treat them as void and will pay only against the Self-Billed Invoice.

3.3 **Tell us of changes to your VAT status.** You must tell Multiplier in writing within [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. 10] days if:

3.3.1 your VAT registration number changes;

3.3.2 you stop being registered for VAT, or become registered for VAT;

3.3.3 you join or leave a VAT group;

3.3.4 you transfer your business, or the part of it that makes the Supplies, to another person; or

3.3.5 any other change affects the VAT treatment of the Supplies or your ability to take part in self-billing.

3.4 **Account for VAT.** You are responsible for accounting to the Tax Authority for any VAT shown on Self-Billed Invoices as charged on your Supplies, and for your own VAT returns and records.

3.5 **Check invoices.** You must check each Self-Billed Invoice and tell Multiplier of any error within [30] days of it being made available.

3.6 **Information.** You must give Multiplier accurate information about your VAT status and evidence of your VAT registration when asked, and keep copies of Self-Billed Invoices as the law requires.

## 4. Errors and disputes

4.1 If either party finds an error in a Self-Billed Invoice, Multiplier will issue a self-billed credit note and, where needed, a replacement Self-Billed Invoice.

4.2 A dispute about the amount payable is resolved under the Adviser Firm Agreement (MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.4). This Agreement deals only with the invoicing of amounts agreed or determined under the Adviser Firm Agreement.

## 6. Term, renewal and ending

6.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 12 months.

6.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the initial term and each renewal term, this Agreement renews automatically for a further 12 months, unless either party gives at least [30] days' written notice before the end of the current term that it will not renew. [OPTION: Where local rules require self-billing agreements to be renewed expressly, Multiplier will ask you to re-sign this Agreement through the Platform before each renewal, and self-billing will stop if you do not.]

6.3 **Ending early.** Either party may end this Agreement at any time on [30] days' written notice. This Agreement ends automatically:

6.3.1 when you stop being registered for VAT, or your VAT status otherwise changes so that self-billing is no longer permitted, unless the parties sign a new self-billing agreement reflecting the change;

6.3.2 when you transfer your business, or the part of it that makes the Supplies; or

6.3.3 when all amounts payable under the Adviser Firm Agreement after its termination have been paid and invoiced.

6.4 **After ending.** When this Agreement ends, Multiplier will issue Self-Billed Invoices only for Supplies made before it ended. For later Supplies, you must issue your own invoices under MPL-ADV-01 clause 12.6.1.

6.5 **Change in VAT status.** Where clause 6.3.1 or 6.3.2 applies, Multiplier may ask you to sign a new version of this Agreement reflecting the change, so that self-billing can continue.
