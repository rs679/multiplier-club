## The key points

This box is a summary. The full terms below apply.

- **Multiplier is a technology platform.** We let you see private-market Products from Sponsors, share information with your adviser and keep your records in one place.
- **We do not give advice.** We do not recommend any Product to you or tell you whether a Product suits you. If you have an adviser, advice comes from your adviser.
- **We screen Sponsors, not investments.** We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product.
- **You make your own decisions.** You must do your own due diligence and read each Product's documents.
- **Your money never comes to us.** If you invest, you contract with the Sponsor and pay the Sponsor, its administrator or an escrow agent directly.
- **We are paid by Sponsors.** Sponsors pay us fees when Investors invest. We share those fees with advisers. Clause 10 explains how.
- **Private-market investments are high risk.** You could lose all the money you invest, and you may not be able to sell your investment.
- **You must be eligible.** You are responsible for making sure you are allowed to invest under the laws of your country. The Platform is not available to US persons.
