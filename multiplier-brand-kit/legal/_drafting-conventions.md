# Drafting conventions for Multiplier platform documents

Every document in this folder follows these rules so they read as one set.

## Header block (top of every document)
```
Document ID: [e.g. MPL-ADV-01]
Version: 0.1 (draft for legal review)
Audience: [who sees it]
Presented when: [the user action that triggers it]
Accepted by: [click-through | checkbox with version log | e-signature | e-signature countersigned by Multiplier]
Unlocks: [what the user can do once accepted]
Re-acceptance: [when a new version must be accepted]
```

## Parties and names
- Multiplier: "Sirius Consulting FZCO, trading as Multiplier, a free zone company incorporated in Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, with licence number 43190, whose registered office is at Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (**Multiplier**, **we**, **us**)".
- Platform: "the Multiplier platform at app.multiplier.club and any related websites, apps and services (the **Platform**)".
- Defined terms in **bold** on first use, then capitalised. Shared definitions: Adviser Firm, Authorised User, Sponsor, Product, Investor, Platform Investor, Deal Fees, Adviser Share, Multiplier Share, Override, Funded Investment, Business Day.

## Fixed positions (do not change)
- Multiplier is a technology platform. It does not give investment, legal or tax advice, does not make personal recommendations, does not hold or receive investor money, and does not underwrite, recommend or endorse any Product.
- Screening wording, used exactly: "We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product."
- Subscription money goes directly to the Sponsor, its administrator or an escrow agent, never to Multiplier.
- The Platform is not available to US persons.
- Advisers are paid 70% of Deal Fees (the **Adviser Share**) by default, paid-when-paid. Overrides for introduced advisers are paid only out of Multiplier's 30% (the **Multiplier Share**), across the levels set out in the Referral Programme Rules.
- Liability exclusions apply "to the fullest extent permitted by law" and never exclude liability for fraud, or anything that cannot lawfully be excluded.
- Never use: "vetted", "verified deal", "approved", "guaranteed", "safe", "best", "AI due diligence". "Verified" refers only to a person's identity or status.
