## Background

A. Multiplier operates the Multiplier platform at app.multiplier.club and any related websites, apps and services (the **Platform**). The Platform connects regulated financial advisers, sponsors and issuers of private-market products, and eligible investors.

B. Before any product is listed on the Platform, Multiplier screens the sponsor and issuer. The Sponsor wishes to apply for screening of the product described in its application.

C. This document sets out the terms of that application, the Sponsor's consent to the checks Multiplier will carry out, and the screening fee.

## 2. What screening is, and what it is not

2.1 Multiplier describes its screening only in these words: "We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product."

2.2 The Sponsor acknowledges that:

2.2.1 Multiplier is a technology platform. It does not give investment, legal or tax advice, does not make personal recommendations, does not hold or receive investor money, and does not underwrite, recommend or endorse any Product;

2.2.2 Screening is carried out for Multiplier's own purposes, to decide whether to make the Platform available to the Sponsor. It is not due diligence on the Product carried out for the Sponsor, any investor, any adviser or anyone else, and no one other than Multiplier may rely on it;

2.2.3 a positive Screening Outcome is not an approval, endorsement, rating, certification or recommendation of the Sponsor, the Issuer or the Product, and does not mean that Multiplier has checked the merits, valuation, legality or documentation of the Product; and

2.2.4 a positive Screening Outcome does not oblige Multiplier to list the Product (see clause 8).

2.3 The Sponsor must not describe the Screening, the Screening Outcome or its relationship with Multiplier in any way other than the wording in clause 2.1 and as permitted by the Content and Marketing Guidelines (PLT-06). In particular, the Sponsor must not describe the Product or the Sponsor as "vetted", "approved", "verified" or "selected" by Multiplier, or use any similar wording.
