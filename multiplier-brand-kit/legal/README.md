**Status: every document in this folder is a first draft (version 0.1) for review by qualified counsel. None of it is legal advice, and nothing here should be published to users, signed or relied on until counsel has reviewed it and the owner has signed off.** Drafting notes (lines starting `> Drafting note:`) are for the reviewing lawyer and must be removed before publishing.

All documents follow [`_drafting-conventions.md`](_drafting-conventions.md). ## Principle: easy to join, gated at the point of action

Sign-up asks for three click-throughs only (PLT-01, PLT-02, PLT-03). Heavier documents appear only when a user takes an action that needs them. Once completed, a document is saved to the user's profile (or firm) and reused; it is asked for again only when its version changes materially, it expires, or it is per Product.

Full document IDs carry the `MPL-` prefix (for example `MPL-PLT-01`). Short IDs are used below.

## Trigger model: what each action requires

Each requirement is asked for only if it is not already complete and current.

### Everyone
| Action | Requires |
|---|---|
| Sign up | PLT-01, PLT-02 (acknowledgement), PLT-03. Email address confirmed, country declared. A US person declaration stops sign-up. |

### Investors
| Action | Requires |
|---|---|
| View a Product's terms or open its data room | INV-01 and INV-02 (once), plus INV-04 for that Product (data room). The Product's country and investor category restrictions must match. |
| Indicate interest or subscribe | Identity verification (and sanctions screening clear) and INV-03 (once, refreshed periodically), then INV-05 for that Product, then the Sponsor's own subscription pack. |

### Advisers
| Action | Requires |
|---|---|
| Request access to a deal, invite a client or share a Product | ADV-01 signed by the Firm Administrator and countersigned (once per firm, incorporating PLT-04 and PLT-06), firm KYB, ADV-05 for each user (with user identity verification), ADV-02 acceptance, and INV-04 for each Product |
| Generate a referral link | ADV-03 |
| Opt in to the leaderboard | ADV-04 |
| Add payout details | ADV-06 where applicable, plus bank account check |

### Sponsors
| Action | Requires |
|---|---|
| Submit a Product for screening | SPN-01, screening fee paid, Sponsor KYB started |
| List the first Product | SPN-02 signed and countersigned (once, incorporating PLT-04 and PLT-06), and ADV-05 for each Sponsor user |
| List each Product | SPN-03 and SPN-04 for that Product. SPN-04 re-signed on any material change and every 12 months while the Product is live |

## Points for the reviewing lawyer and the owner

1. **PLT-04 acceptance model (settled).** PLT-04 is a controller-to-controller schedule incorporated into ADV-01 and SPN-02. Investors are not asked to accept PLT-04. Their permission is captured as a one-tap sharing consent at the moment of action (sharing a profile with an adviser, or subscribing), which names the recipient, is recorded and can be withdrawn in settings; PLT-02 explains it. The CODEX brief reflects this.
2. **Regulatory status wording.** The CODEX brief summarises PLT-01 and INV-01 as "not regulated, no recourse". Following the planning document, PLT-01 instead describes what Multiplier does and does not do, and does not use a blanket "not regulated" or "no recourse" statement. Counsel to settle the regulatory status statement per country.
3. **Overrides (ADV-03, settled).** One level only. The rate depends on the Referring Adviser's tier: 3%, 5% or 10% of Deal Fees. Tiers do not stack; the maximum is 10% and Multiplier keeps at least 20%. Tier qualification measures are still to be set. Counsel should check inducement and disclosure rules.
4. **Document ID format (settled).** All headers use the `MPL-` prefix (for example `MPL-INV-01`); cross-references use the short form.
5. **Consumer investors.** Several limitation, indemnity and arbitration provisions may be unenforceable against consumers in some countries. Counsel to decide whether a consumer addendum is needed.
6. **Placeholders.** Every `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` and `[OPTION A / B]` must be resolved. The planning document (section 5) lists the main commercial and legal decisions.
