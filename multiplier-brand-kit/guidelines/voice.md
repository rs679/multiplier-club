# Voice and copy

## How Multiplier sounds

- **Calm.** Say it once, plainly. No hype, no urgency.
- **Specific.** Numbers, terms and conditions beat adjectives. If a figure is not confirmed, leave a bracketed placeholder such as `[amount]`.
- **Respectful.** The reader is a professional adviser with their own clients and obligations. Explain; never sell at them.
- **Transparent.** Fees, minimums, risks and conflicts are stated where the opportunity is shown.

## Rules

- Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and menus. Mono eyebrows are uppercase by style, typed in sentence case.
- Active voice, short sentences, one idea each. Prefer "We review every deal before it is listed" to "All deals are subject to review".
- Buttons say what happens: "Become a partner", "View deal", "Share with a client". Not "Submit" or "Click here".
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it: "Enter a minimum amount of at least [amount]."
- Avoid: "unlock", "exclusive", "revolutionary", "guaranteed", "best-in-class", exclamation marks, rhetorical questions and emoji.
- Never state or imply expected returns without the approved risk wording from compliance.

## Words to use

| Say | Not |
| --- | --- |
| adviser | broker, agent |
| referral fee | commission, kickback |
| curated, reviewed | handpicked, elite |
| opportunity, deal | product, offer |
| partner | affiliate |
| private markets, alternatives | exotic assets |

## Headline patterns

- A statement of the job: "Access, curated."
- A direct promise of the process: "We select. You introduce. You earn a disclosed fee."
- One emphasised phrase may use `accent`; never more than one per headline.

## Legal and risk copy

- Footers carry the regulatory disclosure and risk warning in `caption`, `ink-subtle`.
- The wording is supplied by compliance and varies by jurisdiction. Components render it; they never write it.
