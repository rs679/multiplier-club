# Logo

## The mark

- Two vesica lenses crossed at 90°, with the overlap cut away. The four remaining tips point to the corners around an empty diamond, an abstract × made as much of absence as of form.
- Construction: four circles of equal radius R, each centred R ÷ 2 from the middle along a diagonal. Opposite pairs overlap to make lenses of exactly 1 : √3 proportion. The overlap is removed. The mark has fourfold rotational symmetry and mirror symmetry on every axis.
- The centre is never filled. Only the four perimeter tips carry colour.

## Colour: black or white, nothing else

- White (`logo-white`, #FFFFFF) on dark and mid-tone grounds: `bg`, `bg-navy`, `action`, photography.
- Black (`logo-black`, #060809) on light grounds: light `bg`, white, paper.
- Use `logo` when the surface follows the theme.
- Never teal or any brand colour, never two-tone, never outlined, never with a filled centre, never a gradient, never with an effect.

## Wordmark

- Set "multiplier" in lowercase Geist Light (weight 300), tracking −4.5%, in the same colour as the mark. It is live text, not a drawn shape.
- The lockup is mark then wordmark, the mark's height equal to the cap-to-descender height of the word, with a gap of 0.3× the mark height. Use the `Logo` component, which sets this up.
- The mark may be used alone for app icons, avatars, favicons and small spaces. The wordmark is never used without the mark except in running text.

## Space and size

- Clear space: half the mark's height on every side. No text, edges or imagery inside it.
- Minimum size: full logo 96px wide on screen (25 mm in print); mark alone 16px (6 mm).

## Placement

- Top left on websites and documents; centred only on covers, cards and sign-off screens.
- App icon: the white mark at 78% of the container on `bg`, container corners `radius-icon`. Avatar: white mark at 60% on `action`.
- Business card: white logo on `bg`; black mark on a light reverse.
