The primary site navigation: logo at the left, links, then actions at the right.

- The consumer provides `links` (`label`, `href`, `current`) and `actions`, normally one `secondary` and one `primary` `Button`. Keep to four links or fewer.
- Sits on `bg-navy` with a 1px `border` underneath. Links are `ink`, the current page is `accent` with `aria-current="page"`.
- The logo always links home. On narrow screens the bar wraps; hide secondary links behind a menu rather than shrinking tap targets below 44px.
- Do not add shadows, transparency or blur.
