The Multiplier mark with or without the lowercase wordmark, only ever solid white or solid black.

- `tone="auto"` (default) follows the theme: white on dark, black on light. Use `tone="light"` or `tone="dark"` when the ground is fixed, such as a photograph or a `action` fill.
- `markOnly` renders the mark alone with the accessible name "Multiplier". Use it for avatars, favicons and app icons.
- `size` is the mark's height in px; the wordmark scales with it. Do not go below 96px wide for the full logo or 16px for the mark.
- Do not set the logo in teal or any brand colour, add outlines, fill the centre or place it on a busy image. Keep clear space of half the mark's height.
- The consumer supplies the ground. On `bg`, `bg-navy` and `action` use light; on light surfaces use dark.
