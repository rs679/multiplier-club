A deal summary card showing asset class, status, title, one-line thesis, minimum and referral fee.

- The consumer provides every fact: `assetClass`, `title`, `summary`, `minimum` and `fee` exactly as they should read, plus an optional `status` word and an `action` such as a `Button`. The component never invents or formats figures; unconfirmed values stay as placeholders like `[amount]`.
- The referral fee is always shown next to the minimum. Do not hide or de-emphasise it.
- `status` is a word ("Open", "Closing soon", "Fully subscribed") with `statusTone` `accent`, `caution` or `critical`.
- Keep `summary` to one or two plain sentences. Never include expected returns here unless compliance has approved the wording.
- Lay cards out in a grid of three at desktop widths, 24px gutters. Use one featured card at most per page.
