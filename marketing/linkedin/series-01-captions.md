# LinkedIn series 01: product and promotional posts

Nine portrait graphics (2160 x 2700, LinkedIn's 4:5 feed size) in `png/`, made from `src/series-01.html` (render with `node src/render.cjs 2`).
Suggested rhythm: two a week, between articles, in the order below. Every screen shows fictional data and is labelled as such.
Rules kept: no fees, commission rates, returns or performance figures; no claims that a deal is checked or endorsed; British English.

## 1. Launch (s01)
Multiplier is open, by invitation.
Sponsors list private market deals. Advisers and adviser firms request terms and distribute them to their clients. Every account is invited, and every listing is reviewed before it appears.
If you advise clients on private markets, or raise capital for them, request access at multiplier.club.

## 2. The Exchange (s02)
Meet the Exchange.
Every listing is anonymised: asset class, sector, region and minimum, with no sponsor or deal name until terms are agreed. One tap requests terms.
Built for advisers who want to see what is available before they commit their time. multiplier.club

## 3. Your Desk (s03)
Your Desk is where your deals live.
Once terms are agreed, the deal moves to your Desk with the sponsor's own summary, the key facts for its asset class, the documents and your questions to us, all in one place. multiplier.club

## 4. For sponsors (s04)
List once. Reach advisers in every country you permit.
Your deal appears anonymised on the Exchange. You see each intermediary's profile (firm or individual, country, client focus) and decide, or let approval run automatically. multiplier.club

## 5. How it works (s05)
Three steps from listing to distribution.
1. The sponsor lists: a summary, the key facts for its asset class and the terms on offer.
2. Multiplier reviews the listing once.
3. Advisers request terms, and the deal sits on their Desk ready to share.
multiplier.club

## 6. No waiting (s06)
No adviser should wait on a sponsor who forgot to sign in.
On Multiplier, approval is automatic by default. Where a sponsor prefers to decide each request, they have two business days, then Multiplier steps in. multiplier.club

## 7. Five fields (s07)
Five fields to list a private equity deal.
People hate forms, so we built ours to ask only what an adviser needs first. Tap instead of typing, write "eur 250k" and it reads EUR 250,000, and add more detail when you have time. multiplier.club

## 8. Our standard (s08)
How we approach listings.
We screen sponsors and issuers for regulatory status and track record before listing. We do not underwrite, recommend or endorse any individual product, and every deal page states whose statement it is. multiplier.club

## 9. Eight asset classes (s09)
Eight asset classes on one Exchange: private credit, loan notes, private equity, venture capital, funds, real estate, infrastructure, and pre-IPO and secondaries.
Each has its own key facts and data room folders, so every deal page answers the first questions an adviser asks. multiplier.club

Add to every post: "For professional advisers and sponsors only. Not an offer or investment advice."
